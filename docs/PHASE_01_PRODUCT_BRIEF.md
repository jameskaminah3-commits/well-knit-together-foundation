# Phase 1 — Product brief and discovery baseline

Status: working draft for foundation review

## Product north star

Well Knit Together should feel like a trusted, calm, beautifully made doorway into support and opportunity. It must work for someone seeking help on a phone with limited data, a donor deciding whether to trust the organisation, a woman presenting her work for sale, and a staff member coordinating services.

This is both:

1. A public story, participation, giving, and marketplace experience.
2. A secure internal operating system for programs, people, money, products, and evidence.

## Product principles

### Dignity before display

People are not campaign assets. Stories, photographs, names, disability information, and child information require clear consent and a real benefit to the person represented.

### Caregiver-centred, not caregiver-exclusive

Women caregivers of children with disabilities are the primary focus, while the platform remains useful for caregivers of other vulnerable groups, vulnerable children, people with disabilities, economically vulnerable women, and the wider community.

### Agency over dependency

Support journeys should lead to options: information, referral, training, peer support, livelihood opportunity, or financial assistance where appropriate.

### Simple for the public, rigorous underneath

The public experience should use plain language and few steps. The staff experience should retain the structure needed for safe triage, follow-up, accountability, and reporting.

### Mobile and low-bandwidth by default

Every essential journey must work on a small screen, support compressed media, avoid unnecessary video, and provide a non-digital contact alternative where a person cannot complete a form.

### Evidence with humility

Impact claims must distinguish between activities, outputs, outcomes, and stories. The platform should never imply that one donation or one intervention guarantees a life outcome.

## Priority audiences and journeys

| Audience | Primary need | First useful action | Success signal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Caregiver seeking help | Understand options without shame | Request support or find a referral | Request is safely received and follow-up expectation is clear |
| Economically vulnerable woman or maker | Learn, present, and sell work | Apply for livelihood support or submit a product | Application/product moves into a transparent review flow |
| Person with disability | Access inclusive information and support | Find a service, resource, or contact route | Content is usable with assistive technology and low bandwidth |
| Parent/guardian of a vulnerable child | Find education, support, and safeguarding information | Explore services or request a referral | Child data is minimised and guardian consent is respected |
| Donor | Decide whether and how to give | Give once, give monthly, or support a campaign | Payment, receipt, and impact expectation are clear |
| Partner or volunteer | Understand fit and next steps | Apply to partner or volunteer | Application is acknowledged and routed to a named owner |
| Staff member | Coordinate work safely | View assigned queue and next action | Every sensitive action is permissioned and auditable |

## Information architecture

### Public navigation

- About: mission, approach, operating footprint, governance, safeguarding, contact.
- Programs: caregiver support; skills and livelihoods; education and advocacy; future program areas.
- Impact: stories, indicators, reports, financial transparency, frequently asked questions.
- Marketplace: products, maker stories, categories, delivery, returns, maker impact.
- Give support: one-time giving, recurring giving, campaigns, payment help, receipts.
- Get involved: partner, volunteer, training, events, resources.
- Support: request help, referral information, privacy, safeguarding, accessible contact options.

### Authenticated navigation

- My profile and preferences.
- My requests/referrals, if applicable.
- My donations and receipts, if applicable.
- My orders and delivery, if applicable.
- Role-specific workspace.

## Phase 1 decisions

- Primary language: English at launch; Swahili content is part of the content workflow, not a later afterthought.
- Primary device: mobile phone.
- Primary visual character: warm, editorial, calm, human, and confident rather than institutional or overly charity-styled.
- Primary conversion actions: request support, donate, buy with purpose, partner, volunteer.
- Public stories: opt-in, consented, reviewable, and separated from case records.
- Public metrics: aggregated and approved; never directly queried from confidential case data.
