# Phase 1 — Content and publishing matrix

The public site should be useful before it is large. Each page needs one owner, one audience, one primary action, and a clear privacy level.

| Surface | Audience | Required content | Primary action | Owner | Privacy level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Everyone | Mission, who we serve, program entry points, trust signals | Explore programs / Give support | Content editor | Public |
| About | Everyone, partners | Mandate, approach, footprint, governance, safeguarding | Partner / Contact | Content editor + admin | Public |
| Program detail | Caregivers, families, partners | Who it is for, what is offered, eligibility, access route, counties | Request support / Learn more | Program manager | Public |
| Support request | Caregivers, families | Plain-language questions, consent, safe contact method | Submit request | Program manager | Confidential |
| Marketplace | Customers, makers | Product images, price, maker story, delivery, returns | Buy product | Marketplace manager | Public; maker story consent required |
| Donate | Donors | Use of funds, giving choices, payment help, receipts, privacy | Donate | Finance + content editor | Public; transaction data confidential |
| Impact | Donors, partners, community | Approved indicators, stories, reports, limitations | Read report / Give | Admin + content editor | Public, aggregated |
| Resources | Caregivers, staff, public | Guides, referral information, training material | Download / Register | Program manager | Public or restricted by item |
| Partner portal | Approved partners | Assigned referrals, agreements, reports, contacts | Update assigned work | Admin | Restricted |
| Staff workspace | Staff | Queues, support records, content, finance, orders, audits | Complete next action | Role owner | Restricted, role-scoped |

## Writing rules

- Use “people with disabilities”, “women caregivers”, and other person-first language unless a community specifically prefers another term.
- Explain acronyms on first use.
- Use short paragraphs, meaningful headings, and specific calls to action.
- Avoid pity, saviour framing, guaranteed-outcome claims, and language that implies a person is valuable only when productive.
- Give users an expectation after every form: what happens next, who may contact them, and when.
- Publish the date and owner for policies, reports, and program information.
- Provide English first drafts with a Swahili translation review process; do not rely on automatic translation for safeguarding or consent copy.

## Story and image checklist

Before publishing a story or image, confirm:

1. The person understands the purpose, audience, channel, and duration of publication.
2. Consent is recorded separately from a service request or donation decision.
3. A guardian process is used for a child, with a best-interest review.
4. The story does not reveal an address, school, medical detail, case status, or other unnecessary identifier.
5. The person can withdraw future use where operationally possible.
6. The final copy was reviewed for dignity, accuracy, and non-exploitative framing.
